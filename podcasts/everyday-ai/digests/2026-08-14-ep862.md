## Topics Covered

### The 70-20-10 AI Budget Rule

BCG's research establishes the "10-20-70 rule" for AI investment: only 10% of effort should go to algorithms, 20% to tech and data infrastructure, and 70% to supporting people and adapting business processes. Despite this, most companies flip the ratio, spending the majority of their budget on tools and licenses while neglecting the human side. BCG's 2025 study of over 1,250 firms worldwide found that only about 5% of organizations have reaped substantial financial gains from AI — and those winners show three-year total shareholder returns roughly four times higher than laggards.

- [AI Transformation Is a Workforce Transformation | BCG](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/ai-transformation-is-a-workforce-transformation) — BCG study showing only 5% of organizations achieve substantial AI financial gains
- [CEO's Guide to Maximizing Value Potential from AI in 2024 | BCG](https://www.bcg.com/assets/2024/executive-perspectives-ceos-guide-to-maximizing-value-from-ai-3july.pdf) — The 10-20-70 rule: algorithms, tech & data, people & processes
- [The Widening AI Value Gap | BCG](https://www.bcg.com/assets/2025/the-widening-ai-value-gap.pdf) — 2025 study of 1,250+ firms; only 5% achieving AI value at scale

### The AI Adoption Crisis: 97% Personal Wins, 29% Enterprise ROI

The WRITER 2026 AI Adoption in the Enterprise survey of 2,400 global employees and C-suite leaders reveals a deep organizational rift: 97% of employees report personal benefits from AI, yet only 23% of companies see significant enterprise-level ROI. Critically, 54% of executives report AI is fracturing their organizations, and 56% say it's causing power struggles. The gap stems from knowledge workers — particularly those with 10-25 years of domain expertise — resisting the loss of agency when AI begins replacing core responsibilities.

- [AI Adoption in the Enterprise 2026 | WRITER](https://go.writer.com/ai-adoption-enterprise-2026) — Survey of 2,400 workers and C-suite leaders; 97% personal wins vs. 23% enterprise ROI
- [Enterprise AI Adoption in 2026: Why 79% Face Challenges Despite High Investment | WRITER](https://writer.com/blog/enterprise-ai-adoption-2026/) — 79% of executives struggle with lagging ROI and strategy gaps
- [WRITER Survey: 60% Plan to Lay Off Employees Who Won't Adopt AI](https://writer.com/blog/enterprise-ai-adoption-survey-results-press-release/) — 60% of companies plan workforce actions against non-adopters

### Rebuild AI-Native: Workflow Redesign Over AI Sprinkling

McKinsey research identified workflow redesign as having the largest EBIT (earnings before interest and taxes) impact across 25 tested attributes of AI deployment. The key insight: bolting AI onto legacy standard operating procedures makes broken processes run faster, not better. Organizations that succeed blow up their existing SOPs and rebuild them from scratch for AI-native workflows — designing processes to be obsolete in months rather than years. Only 11% of companies worldwide are currently using generative AI at scale, according to McKinsey's tech trends research.

- [A Generative AI Reset: Rewiring to Turn Potential into Value in 2024 | McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/a-generative-ai-reset-rewiring-to-turn-potential-into-value-in-2024) — McKinsey on rewiring the business for AI innovation
- [The State of AI in Early 2024: Gen AI Adoption Spikes | McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2024) — Only 5% of companies use gen AI at scale
- [From Promising to Productive: Real Results from Gen AI in Services | McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/from-promising-to-productive-real-results-from-gen-ai-in-services) — Only 3% of North American/European companies have scaled a gen AI use case

### Weekly Enablement Rituals vs. Quarterly Training

Gallup's research shows that employees with AI-supportive managers are nearly twice as likely to use AI frequently and seven times as likely to say AI helps them do what they do best. The podcast argues that AI adoption should be treated as a series of sprints, not a marathon — weekly team rituals (many successful teams anchor on Mondays or Fridays) are essential for sharing what's working, what's not, and adapting to rapid model changes. Quarterly kickoffs and annual seminars build habits, but weekly engagement drives transformation. Gallup's Global Indicator for AI tracks workplace adoption across usage frequency, employee comfort, manager support, and organizational integration.

- [Manager Support Drives Employee AI Adoption | Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/694682/manager-support-drives-employee-adoption.aspx) — Employees with supportive managers are twice as likely to use AI frequently, 7x more likely to say it helps
- [AI's Effect on Workplace Culture | Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/712976/ai-effect-workplace-culture.aspx) — 50% of CHROs lack confidence in managers' ability to guide AI use
- [Global Indicator: Artificial Intelligence | Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/699797/indicator-artificial-intelligence.aspx) — Gallup's ongoing workplace AI adoption tracking

### Big Tech: Tying Performance Reviews to AI Usage

Major tech companies are formalizing AI accountability in performance evaluations. Meta officially tied employee performance reviews and bonus systems to AI usage metrics, tracking token consumption and AI tool adoption through internal dashboards. Amazon revised its performance review system to focus on individual accomplishments and introduced badge swipe tracking for corporate employees. Both companies overhauled their review structures in early 2026, with Amazon cutting 30,000+ roles and Meta simplifying its review framework. Microsoft is also nudging employees toward AI adoption, though with less aggressive enforcement.

- [RTO, Performance Reviews: Big Tech's Show-Your-Work Era | Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/tech-companies-employees-prove-productivity-rto-performance-reviews-ai-investments-2026-1) — Amazon tracks badge swipes; Meta uses AI dashboards for reviews

### BBVA: From 250 Leaders to 83% Bankwide Adoption

BBVA provides a concrete case study in scaling AI adoption. The bank trained 250 senior leaders first — including the CEO and chairman — then scaled organically from 3,300 to 11,000 ChatGPT Enterprise licenses. The result: 83% daily active usage across the bank, with employees saving an average of 2.8 hours per week on routine tasks. BBVA's approach centered on secure tools with clear guardrails (reducing shadow AI risk), internal "Champions" and "Wizards" for peer-to-peer learning, and human oversight — proving that employee-driven adoption outperforms top-down mandates.

- [How BBVA is Scaling AI from Pilot to Practice | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/bbva-2025/) — OpenAI case study: 250 leaders trained first, then scaled to 83% weekly active usage
- [BBVA Expands OpenAI Agreement to 11,000 Licenses | BBVA](https://www.bbva.com/en/innovation/bbva-expands-its-agreement-with-openai-to-11000-chatgpt-licences-for-the-banks-employees/) — BBVA official announcement of license expansion
- [How BBVA Built 11,000 AI Power Users Without a Top-Down Mandate | AI2Work](https://ai2.work/blog/how-bbva-built-11-000-ai-power-users-without-a-top-down-mandate/) — BBVA's employee-driven scaling approach and 2.8 hours/week time savings

### JPMorgan: Flat Headcount, Reshaped Workforce

JPMorgan Chase demonstrates how AI can reshape organizational structure without mass layoffs. CEO Jamie Dimon announced at the February 2026 investor meeting that the bank has "huge redeployment plans" for AI-displaced workers. Total headcount remained flat at 318,512, but the composition shifted significantly: operations roles were cut by 4% while client-facing and revenue-generating positions grew. The bank spends roughly £1.5 billion annually on AI development — about 10% of its technology budget — and reports matching savings. Dimon confirmed the bank has already displaced workers through AI and is actively offering them alternative roles.

- [JPM CEO Dimon: AI Reshaping Workforce, "Huge Redeployment" Plans | CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/24/jpm-ceo-jamie-dimon-ai-reshaping-workforce-redeployment.html) — Dimon confirms AI-displaced workers are being offered new roles
- [JPMorgan's Headcount Is Flat. The Workforce Inside It Is Not. | Talent&](https://talentand.ai/newsroom/jpmorgan-ai-workforce-redeployment/) — 318,512 headcount stable but composition shifting from ops to client-facing
- [JPMorgan Restructures Workforce, Cutting Ops Roles by 4% With AI | EdgeN](https://www.edgen.tech/news/stock/jpmorgan-restructures-workforce-cutting-ops-roles-by-4-with-ai) — Operations roles reduced 4% while client roles grew
