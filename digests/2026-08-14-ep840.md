## Topics Covered

### Voice and Mobile Become the Default AI Interface
Jordan Wilson argues that voice and mobile are becoming the primary AI controls, highlighting ChatGPT Voice (powered by GPT-Live) as a standout feature. The full-duplex voice mode lets users talk to agents that can simultaneously listen, respond, and interact with desktop applications via ChatGPT's Computer Use plugin. Voice is framed as a more natural interface that bypasses the typing bottleneck, putting users in a "flow state" with AI. ChatGPT Voice is available on desktop, iOS, and Android for Plus, Pro, Business, Edu, and Enterprise subscribers.

- [ChatGPT Voice | OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001274/) - Official documentation on ChatGPT Voice availability and features
- [ChatGPT Voice | ChatGPT Learn](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/features/voice) - GPT-Live powered voice for Chat, Work, and Codex
- [Computer Use | ChatGPT Learn](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/computer-use) - ChatGPT can see and operate GUIs on macOS and Windows

### Multiplayer AI: Humans and Agents in Shared Workspaces
Two major products launched recently that enable multiplayer AI: xAI's Grok Bot (launched August 11, 2026) and Block's Buzz (launched July 21, 2026). Grok Bot provides always-on AI agents with their own cloud computers that can sign into existing tools and work across apps. Buzz is an open-source, Nostr-based collaboration platform where AI agents and humans work side by side as first-class teammates in shared channels, with native Git integration. Wilson argues that siloed AI thinking is ending and teams need to share skills and workspaces.

### ChatGPT Sites and Vibe Operations
ChatGPT Sites is described as one of the most underutilized features in AI -- a no-code platform for building and hosting web apps and dashboards through natural language, available within ChatGPT Work/Teams. Apps are fully functional, hosted at public URLs, and don't require any backend knowledge. Wilson predicts this will enable "vibe operations" where entire teams can build internal tools, dashboards, and databases without waiting on BI or engineering teams. Sites supports live databases, file uploads, and collaborative editing.

- [Creating and Managing ChatGPT Sites | OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001339-creating-and-managing-chatgpt-sites) - Official guide to building sites in ChatGPT

### Intelligence Getting Cheaper: GPT-5.6 Price Cuts and Model Competition
OpenAI slashed GPT-5.6 Luna's API price by 80% on July 30, 2026 -- just three weeks after its July 9 launch. Luna dropped from $1/$6 to $0.20/$1.20 per million input/output tokens, making it roughly "Sonnet-level" pricing. GPT-5.6 Terra was cut 20%. Meanwhile, competitors like Grok 4, Meta's Muse Spark 1.2, Kimi K3, and Qwen 3.8 deliver 95-99% of Anthropic's model intelligence at 5-30% of the cost. Wilson argues Anthropic is not keeping pace on the price-performance curve and may get squeezed.

### Anthropic Server Capacity Issues
Wilson points to Anthropic's recurring server outages and degraded performance as evidence that the company underestimated AI demand. He contrasts this with OpenAI's early data center investments, which now give OpenAI higher uptime rates. The outages have been documented across 2026, including a major outage on August 28, 2026 caused by an upstream cloud provider issue that affected Claude Code and Claude Cowork. Wilson suggests Anthropic's capacity constraints are a competitive vulnerability.

### AI Backlash and Invisible Watermarking
Anthropic announced invisible watermarking for Claude-generated text to comply with the EU AI Act. The watermarking uses a statistical approach embedded in text generation that survives copy-and-paste, plus a secondary machine-readable provenance layer. Wilson predicts this will fuel a broader AI backlash, with AI content detectors resurging and more people getting "called out" for using AI. He anticipates a counter-trend where "human-made" becomes a premium label. The watermarking applies to Claude models launched in the EU on or after August 2, 2026.

### AI Solving Math Problems
Wilson claims "math is solved" as a prediction, citing OpenAI's unreleased GPT-6 Astra model, which reportedly knocked out 10 decade-old open math problems with published Lean 4 proofs for approximately $2,000 in compute at Sol API rates. The model scored 98% on the FrontierMath Tier 4 benchmark. Wilson predicts this capability will cascade into open models running on consumer devices within the year, potentially with downsides for cryptography. OpenAI published the mathematical advances on their research blog.

### Recursive Self Improvement (RSI) on the Horizon
Wilson closes with RSI as his #1 prediction, citing Sergey Brin's recent hands-on return to Google AI focused on advancing Gemini 4 through recursive self-improvement. Brin reportedly told DeepMind staff to "go all-in" on Gemini and prioritize AI that can improve without human intervention. OpenAI is also pursuing RSI -- GPT-5.6 Sol was used to make smaller models (Luna) smarter and cheaper. Wilson argues RSI accelerates all 18 other predictions on his list.
