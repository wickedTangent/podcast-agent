## Topics Covered

### Replit Design
Replit launched Replit Design, a full creative suite that replaces Canvas. It features ambient intelligence that suggests design variations as you work, and includes direct integration with Mobbin -- a reference library of 600,000 real app screens that normally requires a paid subscription. Users can upload a design system once and everything generated snaps to their brand. The tool supports multiple models including Claude, GPT-5, Gemini, Kimi, and GLM. It is free for all Replit users with no tier gate announced, which is unusual for a model-heavy design tool. Replit is positioning this as a direct competitor to Figma's handoff workflow, aiming to eliminate the export-and-rebuild step between design and engineering.

### ChatGPT Chrome Extension Updates
OpenAI updated the ChatGPT Chrome extension with YouTube video summarization, allowing users to get quick recaps of videos they watch at accelerated speeds. The extension now supports mentioning open tabs and pulling highlighted text directly into the Side Chat. It can also search browsing history -- for example, finding a page read on a specific day. These features require the Chrome extension specifically (not just the desktop app) but have no plan tier restrictions. The update arrived alongside GPT-5.6 and the ChatGPT Work super-app, representing a shift from a destination chatbot to a layer that sits on top of existing browsing activity.
- [ChatGPT's Chrome extension gets YouTube video and tab support](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/07/31/chatgpt-chrome-extension-youtube-tab-support/) - PiunikaWeb coverage

### Meta AI Tasks with Muse Spark 1.1
Meta AI now runs recurring tasks and daily briefings powered by Muse Spark 1.1, Meta's agentic model built for tool use. Users can set up one-time configurations like weekly plans, restock alerts, or kitchen renovation mood boards, and Meta AI will follow through without needing to be reprompted. The model features major gains in tool and computer use, coding, and multimodal understanding. Access is limited to select markets and is available only through the Meta AI app and meta.ai -- not yet on WhatsApp or Instagram. Meta is shifting its positioning from a conversational chatbot to a proactive personal agent, leveraging its unique social graph as a competitive moat.
- [Introducing Muse Spark 1.1](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-meta-model-api) - Official Meta AI blog
- [Meta AI Adds Recurring Tasks, Email and Calendar Connections With Muse Spark 1.1](https://kingy.ai/news/meta-ai-task-automation-muse-spark-1-1/) - Feature details

### Gemini in Google Docs
Gemini can now generate and edit images, diagrams, and infographics directly inside Google Docs using the document's written content as context. It also handles comment threads by summarizing them, drafting replies, and leaving new comments for review. Sections can be rewritten based on feedback. The feature rolled out on July 28, 2026, and requires a paid plan -- either Workspace Standard Plus, Google AI Pro, or Ultra. Image generation is web-only, and comment features require edit access to the document. This addresses a key bottleneck in document workflows: the review cycle with unresolved comments, rather than the drafting itself.
- [Generate and edit visuals with Gemini in Google Docs](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/07/generate-and-edit-visuals-with-gemini-in-Google-Docs.html) - Official Google Workspace blog

### Gemini Spark in Chrome
Google expanded Gemini Spark to desktop Chrome with "Chrome Auto Browse" -- a feature that, with user permission, can use logged-in accounts and saved passwords to handle complex web errands like scheduling apartment viewings, researching flight options, and starting booking processes. Spark protects against prompt injections and hands sensitive actions (like payments) back to the user. The Chrome integration is initially rolling out in the US with paid Gemini plans. Gemini Spark itself expanded to Google AI Pro subscribers in 160+ additional countries. Spark is now available to paid Gemini subscribers (not just Ultra) and functions as a browser-based agent similar to OpenClaw.
- [Gemini Spark: new Chrome browsing integration](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-spark-updates-july-2026/) - Official Google announcement

### Lyria 3.5
Google DeepMind released Lyria 3.5, its newest AI music generation model, on July 29, 2026. It is available in Google Flow Music, a chat-based music creation tool where users can make iterative edits. The model delivers advances across four dimensions: musicality, lyrics that follow prompts more closely, more expressive vocals, and direct control over tempo, drums, bass, and track length. It requires a paid Gemini account and includes commercial use rights. Google trains on licensed data and does not claim ownership of user-generated content, positioning it favorably against Suno and Udio amid ongoing Sony Music litigation. This is the third meaningful Lyria release in six months, signaling Google's commitment to AI music as a product line.
- [Introducing Lyria 3.5 in Google Flow Music](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/lyria-3-5/) - Official Google announcement

### Buzz by Block
Block (formerly Square, parent of Cash App and Square) launched Buzz, a free open-source collaboration platform built on the Nostr protocol. It is a Slack-like workspace where humans and AI agents work together in shared channels. Every participant -- human or agent -- gets a cryptographic identity, meaning every message, code change, and approval leaves a signed trail. Agents join as members with scoped permissions, not as bots waiting on slash commands. Buzz supports channels, threads, direct messages, voice, media sharing, code repositories, and automated workflows. It is open source under Apache 2.0, available via Block's hosted version, desktop app, self-hosted option, and mobile apps (iOS and Android). Out of the box, it works with Claude Code, Codex, and Block's own agent, but can support any model through integrations like OpenRouter.
